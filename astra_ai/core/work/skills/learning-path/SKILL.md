# Learning path

## Gather (in parallel)
- Courses: free university courses on the subject with their lecture pages, syllabus and assignments.
- Books: the standard free or open textbooks and notes, with their official pages.
- Videos: full lecture series or the best single lectures, with each video's link and length.
- Use the person's level and time per week when given; otherwise a motivated beginner at about six hours a week.

## Result
- The material, grouped as courses, books and videos, each with why it is in the path.
- A week-by-week plan: each week names what to read or watch and what to practise.
- Where to start today.
