# Competitor landscape

## First
- Know the product and its market. When the request names only a category, find the five to eight players that matter.

## Gather (in parallel)
- Players: who competes (named ones, or the leaders and fastest-growing newcomers), founding year, funding and audience when published.
- Pitch: each competitor's own home and pricing pages: the hero headline verbatim, the category they claim, the entry price and free plan.

## Result
- A landscape table: Company, Positioning, For, Starts at (with currency and period), Free plan, two to four capabilities that split the market, Raised. Unknown stays empty.
- "How they pitch": each competitor's headline exactly as its page shows it, with the page cited.
- Where the opening is, with up to three messages to test, each tied to a row of the table.
