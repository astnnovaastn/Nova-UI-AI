# Deep research

## Gather
- A page you can name that holds the answer (the official page, the statistic's source, the paper) is read first.
- Otherwise search from several angles in one round: one short query per part of the request, each naming the subject and the one thing needed. Never search again in other words for what a search did not find.
- Prefer primary sources (the company's own page, the paper, the official statistic) over summaries of them.
- For wide subjects, give each analyst one thread (costs, safety, market…) so their findings stay separate.

## Check
- Cross-check every figure and date against a second source when one exists; mark single-source claims.
- Gaps found by verification become targeted follow-up searches, at most one round.

## Result
- A report whose sections follow the request: an executive summary, one section per part of the request, contradictions and open questions, limitations, conclusions, bibliography.
- A table wherever several subjects share comparable facts; numbers keep their units and dates.
- Every claim cites its source; say plainly what the sources do not establish.
