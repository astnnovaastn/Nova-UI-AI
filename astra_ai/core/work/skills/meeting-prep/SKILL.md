# Meeting prep

## Gather
- People: the attendees' public roles and companies, and each company's news from the last three months. The person's own text never goes into a search: only names and companies do.

## Result
- Who is coming, each with role, company and the one fact that matters here.
- What to raise: at most seven points, each tied to its source.
- AEGIS cannot read the person's calendar or mail; it says so when asked to.
