# Investor list

## First
- Know the company's space, stage, round size and location. Ask only what is missing, as one question.

## Gather (in parallel)
- Funds that led or joined rounds at this stage in this space in the last eighteen months, each with the partner who led the deal, the deal and its date, and the check size when published.
- Angels (pre-seed and seed): operators who backed similar companies recently, with the deal and its date.

## Result
- The best six to ten fits, each with what they are, check size, stage, the relevant deal and why they fit.
- A full table only for more than ten.
- Only deals from sources, with their dates.
