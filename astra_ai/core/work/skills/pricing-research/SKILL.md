# Pricing research

## Gather
- The competitors the request names, else the five or six that matter.
- Read each one's own pricing page by its plain address. A price from anywhere but the vendor's own page is marked as such.

## Result
- A table, one row per product: Model (per seat, usage, flat), Free plan, Free limit, Entry price, Next plan, Annual discount, Enterprise. Values exactly as the pages show them, with the month read.
- The median entry price and the range.
- A recommendation drawn from the table: where the market clusters and what a free plan must include to compete.
- Never a remembered price.
