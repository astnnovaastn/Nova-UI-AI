# Market map

## Gather (in parallel)
- Players: the companies in the market from recent sources (analyst maps, funding news, category pages), each with its site, founding year, total raised and last round with its date, and the one line it sells.
- Segments: how the market divides (by customer, approach or layer) as companies and analysts describe it.

## Result
- The segments (at most six) with the companies in each (at most twenty-four overall: leaders and notable newcomers).
- A facts table: Company, Segment, Founded, Raised, Last round, Link, sorted by raised.
- The gap worth a company, and consolidation, pricing pressure or platform threats only when sources show them.
- Only companies from sources; an unknown figure stays empty.
