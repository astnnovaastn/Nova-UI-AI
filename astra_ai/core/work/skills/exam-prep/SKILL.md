# Exam prep

## First
- Know the course or subject, the exam date and the time per day. Ask only what is missing.

## Gather (in parallel)
- Syllabus: the course's own page or the standard syllabus; topics and their weight when published.
- Past papers: past exams with solutions for this course or the closest one.

## Result
- A plan from today to the exam, one session per day, hardest and heaviest topics first, mock exams two or three days before.
- The best material and past papers, each with why it is in the plan.
- Ten practice questions on the heaviest topics, each with a short answer.
